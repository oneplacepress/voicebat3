Our Pitch Deck

Our Purpose

🗣️ Improve Messaging

Adding VOICE & personality to texts to make communication more expressive and meaningful.

📡 IoT Integration

Help people utilize IoT devices seamlessly with voice-enabled communication systems.

🌐 DIY Networks

Assist communities in building Do-It-Yourself Wide-Area-Networks based on LoRa wireless standard.

The Problem

🦇 Texts Lack Voice

Texts are dispassionate & dull – without the emotion & audible 'clues' contained in voices – texts can be misunderstood.

⚠️ Dangerous Distractions

Texting is distracting & dangerous – especially when eyes & attention need to be focused on other things.

📵 Coverage Limitations

Without cell-phone coverage or WiFi – mobile messaging is nearly impossible except for expensive satellite systems.

Our Solution

🦇 Both Audio & Text (BAT)

Give users the option to read – or listen – to what is being said – by including Both Audio & Text in messages.

🎙️ Push-To-Talk

Eliminate distractions by employing Push-To-Talk – users hold down a mic button to talk without looking at a screen.

📡 LoRa Mesh Networks

Remove dependence on wireless providers by transmitting BAT messages via inexpensive LoRa mesh networks.

Why Now?

🗣️ Voice Matters

People want their VOICE to be heard – texts simply can't compare – as words are only mediocre subtitles next to a personal VOICE.

💭 Natural Expression

It's EASIER to vocalize feelings than to write them in words & emoji.

🤖 AI & LoRa Advances

Advances in AI & LoRa make it possible for people to use their VOICE without always relying on wireless providers.
