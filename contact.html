Frequently Asked Questions

🦇 What is VoiceBat?

VoiceBat is a revolutionary communication system that combines Both Audio & Text (BAT) in messages sent over LoRa mesh networks, giving users voice and personality without cellular dependence.

📻 How does SONAR MINI work?

SONAR MINI is a wearable device that captures 30-second voice messages, automatically transcribes them, and transmits both audio and text over LoRa networks up to 2 miles without cellular coverage.

💰 What are the pricing options?

We offer Starter Kits ($200), Professional Kits ($1,500), and Community Kits ($5,000). Individual SONAR MINI devices are $100 each. Contact us for custom enterprise solutions.
