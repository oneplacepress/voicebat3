Meet the Founders

🤝 Mike & Jay's Journey

VoiceBat was founded by Mike and Jay, who met through Y-Combinator's co-founder matching program. Combining their expertise and shared vision, they joined forces to create VoiceBat—a platform dedicated to transforming the way people communicate by adding voice and personality to messaging.

Their complementary skills and shared passion for human-centered technology made them the perfect team to tackle one of communication's biggest challenges: restoring the human element to digital messaging.

👨‍💼 Mike's Background

Technical expertise in wireless communication and IoT systems. Passionate about building resilient networks that empower communities.

🧠 Jay's Background

Product design and user experience specialist focused on voice-first communication experiences that feel natural and human.

Our Mission

🎯 Empowering Communication

Our mission is to empower individuals and communities with innovative tools that bridge the gap between text and voice, making communication more expressive and accessible.

🌐 Community Independence

We believe communities should control their own communication infrastructure. VoiceBat enables local mesh networks that don't depend on big tech companies.

🦇 Voice & Personality

Technology should enhance human expression, not diminish it. We're restoring the emotional richness of voice to digital communication.

The Y-Combinator Connection

🤝 Co-Founder Matching

Mike and Jay discovered each other through Y-Combinator's prestigious co-founder matching program, designed to connect complementary entrepreneurs with shared visions.

💡 Shared Vision

Both founders were independently frustrated by the limitations of text-only communication and the dependence on cellular networks. Their meeting sparked the VoiceBat concept.

🚀 Building Together

Combining Mike's technical expertise with Jay's user experience background, they created the perfect team to build revolutionary communication technology.

Our Values

🗣️ Authentic Expression

We believe human communication should never lose its most essential element: the human voice. Every feature we build serves authentic expression.

🔓 Open Innovation

Technology should be accessible and community-controlled. We build open systems that empower users rather than lock them in.

🌟 Simple Excellence

Complex technology should feel simple. We obsess over user experience to make powerful communication tools accessible to everyone.
